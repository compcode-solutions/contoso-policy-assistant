# Mail and Internal Post

Incoming post is sorted at reception and placed in floor trays by 14:00 on business days.

- Personal parcels should go to a home address.
- Restricted documents in paper form move in sealed internal envelopes.
- Outbound courier jobs are booked through facilities, not a personal account.
- Uncollected tray items older than 14 days are returned to the sender's department.

Do not leave open customer files on the mail counter.
