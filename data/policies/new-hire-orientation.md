# New Hire Orientation

New joiners complete orientation within **ten working days** of start.

- Complete the handbook acknowledgement in the HR portal.
- Finish mandatory modules: conduct, data handling, and site induction.
- Collect a badge from reception; laptops are issued by IT on the start date or the next business day.
- A buddy is assigned by the hiring manager for the first four weeks.

Missing modules block system access beyond the starter set.
