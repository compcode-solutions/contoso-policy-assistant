# Contoso Code of Conduct

Everyone who works at Contoso is expected to treat colleagues, customers, and suppliers with courtesy.

- Do not harass, bully, or discriminate.
- Do not accept gifts above a token value without recording them with your manager.
- Raise concerns through your manager or the confidential reporting channel.
- Follow local law and this handbook even when away from the assigned office.

Breaches are reviewed under the applicable people process. Contact hr@contoso.example.
