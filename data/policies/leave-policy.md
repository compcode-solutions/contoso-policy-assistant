# Contoso Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive **20 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.

- Leave requests must be submitted at least **5 business days** in advance when possible.
- Unused leave may carry over up to **5 days** into the next year with manager approval.
- Sick leave is tracked separately (see HR portal).

For questions, contact hr@contoso.example.
