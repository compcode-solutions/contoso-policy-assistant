# Workplace Safety Escalation SOP

This document is for **Supervisors** and above.

If a safety incident is reported on site:

1. Ensure people are safe; call emergency services if needed.
2. Notify the site Safety Officer within **1 hour**.
3. **Escalate** by creating a Priority-1 ticket in the incident system with: location, time, people involved, immediate actions.
4. Do not discuss details on public channels.
5. Preserve photos/logs for investigation.

Failure to escalate a known serious hazard is a policy violation.
