# Speaking Up

Anyone may report suspected fraud, safety cover-ups, or legal breaches without using the management chain.

- Use the independent hotline or speakup@contoso.example.
- Good-faith reports are protected from retaliation.
- You may remain anonymous where the law allows.
- HR acknowledges receipt and says whether the matter will be investigated.

This channel is not for ordinary IT tickets or facilities faults.
