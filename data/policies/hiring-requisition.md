# Hiring Requisition

Supervisors raise backfill and growth seats in the recruiting system. They do not make verbal offers.

- Attach the current job description and the cost centre.
- Backfills of the same grade need the people partner only.
- Grade changes or new seats need Finance and the director.
- Agency use requires a preferred-supplier record; do not engage a new firm by email.

Interview feedback is entered in the system within two working days of the interview.
