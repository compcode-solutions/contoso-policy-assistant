# Supervisor On-Call Coverage

Each site publishes a supervisor on-call roster four weeks ahead.

- The on-call supervisor answers the duty phone within **20 minutes**.
- Customer severity-1 events are conferenced to the incident commander, not handled only in chat.
- Handover notes are written before coming off shift.
- Swaps are recorded on the roster; informal swaps are not recognised for pay.

On-call is not a substitute for a filled night-shift supervisor seat.
