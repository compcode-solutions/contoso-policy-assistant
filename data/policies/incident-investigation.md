# Incident Investigation

After a reported workplace event, the supervisor opens an investigation file within **one business day**.

- Interview involved people separately. Do not group-interview.
- Preserve CCTV requests through Security; do not copy footage to laptops.
- Root-cause notes use the five-why template on the Safety share.
- Close the file or escalate to the site Safety Officer within **five business days**.

Staff may be told that a review is underway. They are not given draft findings.
