# Disciplinary Procedure

This procedure is for **Supervisors** and People partners. It is not circulated to individual contributors.

For conduct or performance issues:

1. Hold a private fact-finding conversation and keep notes.
2. Issue a **documented verbal caution** if the issue is first-time and minor.
3. A **first written notice** follows if the issue repeats within twelve months.
4. A **final written notice** is the last step before a termination recommendation.

HR must review any written notice before it is given. Do not discuss cases in open chat channels.
