# Executive Travel Exception

Business class, suites, and non-preferred carriers for officers require an Admin exception file.

- The standard travel policy still applies to everyone else, including directors without an exception.
- Exceptions are named, dated, and expire; they are not blanket.
- Companion travel is never paid by the company.
- Billing is coded to the executive cost centre, not a team envelope.

This file is not a perk catalogue. It exists so Finance can reconstruct approvals.
