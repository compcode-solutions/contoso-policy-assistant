# Acceptable Use of Systems

Company email, chat, and network access are for work.

- Modest personal use is allowed if it does not interfere with duties or consume unusual bandwidth.
- Do not send confidential files to personal email.
- Do not run cryptocurrency miners, scanners, or unapproved remote-access tools.
- Phishing reports go to security@contoso.example — do not forward the message to colleagues.

Monitoring of systems is described in the privacy notice on the intranet.
