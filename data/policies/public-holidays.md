# Public Holidays

Offices follow the published holiday calendar for the country of the assigned workplace.

- The calendar is posted each November for the following year.
- Sites that must run on a holiday use a volunteer roster published four weeks ahead.
- Floating cultural days are requested in the time system like any other planned absence.
- Holiday shutdowns of plants are announced by Operations, not assumed from the office calendar.

Shift workers see site-specific notes on the Operations board.
