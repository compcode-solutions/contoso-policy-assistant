# Overtime Approval Limits

Supervisors may approve overtime up to **10%** of a team's scheduled weekly hours.

- Above 10% in any week requires the next-level manager.
- Plant shutdown recovery overtime needs Operations Director sign-off.
- Do not approve overtime to cover an unfilled vacancy for more than four consecutive weeks; raise a requisition instead.
- Reject after-the-fact overtime unless a safety or customer-outage reason is recorded.

Finance publishes the weekly overtime dashboard each Tuesday.
