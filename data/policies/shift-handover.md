# Shift Handover

Plant and warehouse supervisors complete a written handover at every shift change.

- Open incidents, isolation locks, and visitor contractors are listed by name of the controlling person.
- Incomplete work is either closed or explicitly accepted by the incoming supervisor.
- Verbal-only handovers are not accepted on nights or weekends.
- The outgoing supervisor stays until the incoming supervisor signs the sheet.

Lost handover sheets are a process failure and are recorded, not ignored.
