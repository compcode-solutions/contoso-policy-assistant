# Building Evacuation

If the fire alarm sounds, leave by the nearest stair. Do not use lifts.

- Go to the posted assembly area for your floor.
- Remain there until facilities gives an all-clear.
- Assist visitors you brought on site.
- Do not re-enter to collect bags.

Wardens are listed on each floor plate. This note does not replace site emergency plans.
