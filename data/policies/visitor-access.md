# Visitor Access

All visitors sign in at reception and wear a visitor badge on site.

- Hosts stay with visitors in restricted areas.
- Contractors working more than five days use a contractor badge instead.
- Photography of production areas is not allowed without facilities approval.
- Visitors leave by the published site close time unless a night-work permit exists.

Do not badge a visitor through a staff-only door.
