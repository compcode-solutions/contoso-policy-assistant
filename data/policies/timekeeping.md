# Timekeeping and Attendance

Non-exempt staff record actual hours in the time system by **Monday 10:00** for the prior week.

- Clock in and out for unpaid lunch breaks of 30 minutes or more.
- Missed punches must be corrected by the employee and acknowledged by the manager.
- Repeated unexplained lateness may lead to coaching.
- Do not share badge IDs or clock in for another person.
