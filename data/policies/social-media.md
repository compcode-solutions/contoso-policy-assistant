# Personal Social Media

Personal accounts are personal. Do not present them as official Contoso channels.

- Do not post Internal or Restricted material.
- Product complaints from customers are routed to support, not answered in comment threads by staff.
- If you mention the employer, a simple "opinions my own" note is enough.
- Official posts are made only by Corporate Communications.

Media requests are forwarded to press@contoso.example.
