# Employee Wellness Program

Contoso offers a voluntary wellness stipend of **$40 USD per month** toward gym, mental-health apps, or similar.

- Enrolment is through the benefits portal each January or at hire.
- Receipts are not required for the stipend; unused amounts do not carry over.
- Participation is optional and is not used in performance ratings.
- Occupational health referrals for work-related strain are a separate process.

For medical leave, use the HR portal, not this program.
