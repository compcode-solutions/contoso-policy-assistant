# Contoso Travel Policy

Business travel must be booked through the Contoso travel desk.

- Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy allowed above 6 hours with manager approval.
- Hotel cap: **$200** per night in tier-1 cities unless pre-approved.
- Book at least **7 days** ahead when practical.
- Combine meetings to reduce trips when possible.
