# Volunteer Time

Full-time staff may take **one working day** per calendar year for a registered charity, unpaid by the charity.

- Request the day in the time system at least one week ahead.
- The organisation must be a registered non-profit in the country of the workplace.
- Party-political campaigning is not covered.
- Unused days do not carry forward.

Team volunteering events are organised by HR and do not use this day.
