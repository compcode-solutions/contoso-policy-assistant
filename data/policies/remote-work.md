# Contoso Remote Work Guidelines

Eligible roles may work from home up to **three days** per week with manager agreement.

- Core hours are **10:00–16:00** in the employee's assigned time zone.
- A dedicated, reasonably private workspace is required for customer calls.
- Company equipment remains company property and must be returned on request.
- Occasional extra remote days need manager approval in the time system.

This is not a substitute for parental or medical absence rules.
