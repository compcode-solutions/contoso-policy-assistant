# Vendor Master Onboarding

New suppliers are added to the vendor master only after Admin finance checks.

- Tax and bank details are verified by a second person before the first payment.
- Preferred-supplier status is not implied by a single statement of work.
- Sanctions screening is completed before the vendor ID is issued.
- Changes to bank details require a call-back to a number already on file, never the number in the change email.

Buyer teams submit requests; they do not edit the master file.
