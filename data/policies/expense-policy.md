# Contoso Expense Policy

Employees may claim reasonable business expenses with receipts.

- The daily meal limit while traveling is **$60 USD** per day.
- Local client meals require pre-approval above **$100**.
- Submit expenses within **30 days** of the spend date.
- Personal expenses are not reimbursable.

Approvers: people managers for under $500; Finance for $500+.
