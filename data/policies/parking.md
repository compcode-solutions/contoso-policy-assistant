# Parking and Site Access

Assigned lots are for registered vehicles only.

- Register the plate in the facilities portal before the first on-site day.
- Overnight parking needs a facilities ticket.
- Do not block loading docks or emergency routes.
- Lost badges are reported to reception the same day.

Visitor parking is marked separately and is first-come.
