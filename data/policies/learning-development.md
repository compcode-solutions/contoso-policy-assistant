# Learning and Development

Employees may use **two working days** per year for structured training relevant to the role.

- Book time in the learning system so coverage can be planned.
- External courses over the published cap need manager and Finance approval.
- Internal catalogues and recorded talks do not count against the two days.
- Conference attendance is booked through the usual trip process, not this policy.

Share notes with the team when the company paid for a seat.
