# Site Inspection Checklist

Supervisors walk their area using this list at least **monthly** and file the signed sheet.

- Exits unobstructed; extinguishers tagged in date.
- Chemical cabinets locked; spill kit sealed.
- Guarding in place on running equipment.
- Near-miss cards from the month reviewed with the team.

Failed items that cannot be fixed the same day are logged to Facilities with a target date. Repeat fails in the same category are raised to the Safety Officer.
