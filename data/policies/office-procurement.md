# Office Supplies and Small Purchases

Stationery and standard peripherals are ordered from the preferred catalogue.

- Desk items under the catalogue list price do not need a purchase order.
- Non-catalogue items need manager sign-off in the procurement form.
- Do not buy furniture or appliances on a personal card expecting reimbursement.
- Return unused bulk orders to facilities rather than storing them at home.

Preferred vendors are listed on the intranet procurement page.
