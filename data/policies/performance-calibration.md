# Performance Calibration

Year-end ratings are calibrated in a manager-only session before they are released to staff.

- Bring written examples, not recency anecdotes.
- Forced ranking is not used; the session checks consistency across teams.
- Do not promise a rating to an employee before calibration closes.
- Compensation recommendations are collected separately and are not discussed in the rating room.

Session notes stay in the manager workspace. They are not stored on shared drives.
