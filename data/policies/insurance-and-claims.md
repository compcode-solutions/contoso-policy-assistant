# Insurance and Claims

Property, liability, and travel-medical claims are filed by Admin Finance, not by site supervisors.

- Notify the broker within **two business days** of a potentially insured event.
- Do not admit liability to a third party.
- Repair quotes above the published deductible need the claims handler's go-ahead before work starts.
- Policy wordings and limits are not posted on the employee intranet.

Site teams collect facts and photos; they do not contact the insurer.
