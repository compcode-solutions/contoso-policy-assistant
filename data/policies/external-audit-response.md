# External Audit Response

Requests from external auditors are coordinated by the Admin control owner.

- Do not send raw system extracts to an auditor from a personal mailbox.
- Sample selections and PBC lists live in the audit workspace.
- Management comment letters are drafted by Finance and signed by the designated officer.
- Findings with a due date under 30 days are tracked daily until closed.

Staff interviews with auditors are scheduled; walk-ups are redirected to the control owner.
