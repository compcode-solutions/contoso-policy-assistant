# Data Handling Basics

Label files as **Public**, **Internal**, or **Restricted** before sharing.

- Public: marketing pages and published job ads.
- Internal: ordinary project notes, team calendars, non-sensitive process docs.
- Restricted: customer lists, unpublished financials, health information, authentication material.
- Restricted files stay in approved stores. Do not put them on USB drives or personal cloud.

When unsure, treat the file as Internal and ask your manager.
