# Team Operating Budget

Each supervisor receives a quarterly operating envelope for tools, local trips, and team events.

- The envelope is visible in the finance manager pack, not on the employee intranet.
- Team events stay under the published per-head cap unless Finance pre-approves.
- Software that processes customer data is not bought from this envelope; use Security review.
- Unused budget does not automatically carry to the next quarter.

Forecast changes are submitted by the 10th of the middle month of the quarter.
