# Contoso Laptop & Device Policy

Company laptops are issued for work use.

- Devices must use disk encryption and company MDM enrollment.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within **24 hours**.
- Do not store customer PII on the local desktop; use approved cloud stores.
- Personal software installs require IT approval.

Request a replacement via the IT portal.
