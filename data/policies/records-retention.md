# Records Retention Schedule

Retention periods are set by record class. Admin owns the schedule.

- Personnel files: duration of employment plus the published statutory period.
- Contracts: term plus seven years unless Counsel marks a longer class.
- CCTV: 30 days unless a hold or incident file exists.
- Destruction is logged; informal deletion of scheduled records is not allowed.

Department heads may not shorten a class to free up storage.
