# Compensation Administration

Pay band midpoints and merit matrices are maintained by People Operations. They are **Admin-only**.

- Band midpoints must not be forwarded to people managers as a spreadsheet.
- Offers above 110% of midpoint need the compensation committee.
- Equity refresh modelling is run twice a year and is not discussed in ordinary calibration.
- Exception payments are logged in the compensation ledger with a named approver.

Leaking band tables to staff is a policy breach.
