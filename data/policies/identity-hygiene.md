# Account and Identity Hygiene

Each person uses their own directory account. Sharing accounts is not allowed.

- Enable the company multi-factor prompt on the first login.
- Lock the workstation when stepping away.
- Do not write sign-in secrets on paper or in chat.
- Report a suspected account takeover to IT within one hour of noticing it.

Password resets are self-service on the identity portal. IT will never ask you to read a secret aloud.
