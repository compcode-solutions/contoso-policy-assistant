# Workplace Appearance

Contoso uses a **business casual** standard at headquarters and customer sites.

- Closed-toe shoes are required in warehouses, labs, and plant floors.
- Customer-facing meetings follow the host's dress expectation if stricter.
- Branded clothing is optional except at trade shows.
- Religious dress and medically required items are accommodated.

Ask your manager if a site has additional PPE rules.
