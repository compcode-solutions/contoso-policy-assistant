# Legal Hold

When Counsel issues a hold, named custodians must not delete or alter potentially relevant records.

- Hold notices are sent only by Counsel or a designated Admin.
- IT places mailbox and drive holds; supervisors do not run these jobs.
- Release of a hold is written, never assumed because a case went quiet.
- Questions from custodians go to Counsel, not to the employee's manager.

Discussing the existence of a hold outside the need-to-know list is prohibited.
