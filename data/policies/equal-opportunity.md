# Equal Opportunity

Hiring, pay decisions, and promotions are based on role requirements and demonstrated performance.

- Protected characteristics under applicable law are not used as selection criteria.
- Job ads state essential requirements only.
- Interview panels use the same question set for a given requisition.
- Accommodation requests go to hr@contoso.example.

This policy applies to employees, applicants, and on-site contractors.
