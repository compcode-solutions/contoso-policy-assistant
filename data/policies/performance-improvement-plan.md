# Performance Improvement Plan

A PIP is used when a role is still wanted but output is below the documented standard.

- Duration is typically **30, 60, or 90 days**, agreed with HR before the employee meeting.
- Goals must be observable. Do not use attitude language without examples.
- Check-ins are weekly and written.
- Ending a PIP without HR review is not permitted.

A PIP is not a substitute for the disciplinary procedure when the issue is misconduct.
