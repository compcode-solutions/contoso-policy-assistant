# Overtime for Hourly Staff

Overtime is hours beyond the scheduled week, pre-approved in the time system.

- Work the extra hours only after the manager's approval is visible on the timesheet.
- Overtime is paid per local law; time-in-lieu is used only where the handbook says so.
- Do not stay late "off the clock".
- Weekend plant coverage is scheduled, not assumed.

Exempt (salaried) roles do not accrue overtime under this policy.
