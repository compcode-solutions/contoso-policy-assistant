# Meeting Rooms and Shared Spaces

Book rooms in the calendar system. Unbooked rooms may be used for 15 minutes, then released.

- End on time so the next meeting can start.
- Wipe boards and take printed matter with you.
- Food in rooms is allowed; leave the room clean.
- Do not override a booking by removing someone else's hold.

AV faults are logged with facilities, not by unplugging racks.
